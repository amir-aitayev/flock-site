Questions

When can we use it?

Flock opens in Rome and Milan first. Join the waitlist and we write once, the day it opens in your city.

How do we sign in?

With a six‑digit code sent to your email, or with Sign in with Apple.

Who can see our flat?

Only the people in it. Turns, money and chat stay inside your flat.

What if I tap the wrong thing?

Most actions show an Undo for a few seconds instead of asking whether you are sure. Only steps that delete something ask first.

We speak different languages.

Chat is translated, so each of you reads the flat chat in your own language.

iPhone or Android?

Both, with a light and a dark appearance that follows your phone.

What is the house pact?

The few rules your flat agrees on, like quiet hours and the shelf in the fridge, kept where everyone can see them.
