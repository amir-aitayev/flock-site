A flat is a small flock

Living together works when everyone pulls their weight and helps each other out. Flock makes the helping easy and the fairness automatic.

Ask the flat

A drill, a quiet night before an exam, a hand with a sofa. Ask once, and flatmates answer in one tap.

One list, two halves

A shared list for the flat and a personal one only you see. Split what you bought, or keep it as your own expense.

Notes on Today

Pin a heads‑up for everyone, the plumber on Friday or the broken oven. It comes down by itself, and it can go without your name.

Everything a shared flat runs on

One app for the whole flat, in the colours you’ll see on your phone.

Chores that go round

Bathroom, kitchen, rubbish: say how often, and Flock rotates whose turn it is. Each one is ticked off on its day, never early.

The washing machine

See which machine is free and who’s using it, and get in line for the washer. Nobody queues for the dishwasher.

One calendar for chores and events

The flat’s chores and your own events side by side. Your events stay yours.

Money that stays square

Add a bill and split it with everyone or just some. Expenses sort themselves into folders: rent, bills, groceries, eat out, or your own.

Your own bird

A photo, your initials, or one of twelve Flock birds in your colour.

Six ways to ask the flat

Quiet please, a party, a guest staying, borrowing, a hand, or swapping a turn: each answered in one tap.

Questions

When can we use it?

Flock opens in Rome and Milan first. Join the flock below and we write once, the day it opens in your city.

How do we sign in?

With a six‑digit code sent to your email, or with Sign in with Apple.

Who can see our flat?

Only the people in it. Turns, money and chat stay inside your flat, and your landlord can’t see them. If we ever share numbers with a housing company, they will only cover flats that opted in, as totals that can’t identify a flat or a person.

What’s private to me?

Your personal shopping list, your events on the calendar and your personal expenses. Flatmates never see them.

What if I tap the wrong thing?

Most actions show an Undo for a few seconds instead of asking whether you are sure. Only steps that delete something ask first.

Which languages?

English and Italian. Each flatmate picks their own.

iPhone or Android?

Both, with a light and a dark look that follows your phone.
